INCIDENT REPORT - COMPRESSOR C-202 HIGH TEMPERATURE TRIP
Date of incident: 2026-07-08
Department: Operations
Reported by: Shift operator

Summary: Compressor C-202 tripped on a high discharge temperature of 110 C during afternoon operation. Ambient temperature was 41 C.

Findings: The oil cooler fins were heavily fouled with dust. The cooling fan was running at reduced speed and the oil level was slightly below the minimum mark.

Corrective actions: The oil cooler was cleaned, the oil level was topped up and the cooling fan was inspected. Compressor C-202 was restarted after a successful test run.

Root cause assessment: A fouled oil cooler is the most probable cause of the overheating. This is an assessment, not a confirmed root cause. A similar overheating event occurred on 2026-04-17.

Safety: Operators must not open the compressor enclosure while the unit is hot.
