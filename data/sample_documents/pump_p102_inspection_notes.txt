INSPECTION NOTES - COOLING WATER PUMP P-102
Date: 2026-05-18

Vibration analysis of Pump P-102 showed vibration of 3.1 mm/s, which is within acceptable limits. Minor leakage was noted at the gasket of the discharge flange and the gasket was tightened.
